# Title

## The idea

What we want, in plain language — the kind of description you would give someone over
coffee. No file names, no APIs, no code.

## Why

The problem this solves, or the reason it is worth the effort. What can't be done today.

## What good looks like

Concrete, observable signs that this is finished and worth having. Prefer things a person
can check by using the thing over things only visible in the source.

## Constraints

limits we are not going to argue with later. Naming a technology here is fine.

## Not in scope

they went somewhere (another intent, a later round).

don't pad it.
